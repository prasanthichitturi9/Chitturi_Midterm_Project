# AI Usage Overview

**Project:** Comparison of Three NLP Models  
**AI Tool Used:** ChatGPT

I used ChatGPT to obtain Python code examples and guidance for implementing the Bigram, LSTM, and Transformer models.

I personally executed the code in Google Colab, reviewed the outputs, and analyzed the training and validation results of all three models.

I wrote my own observations and conclusions based on the experimental results. ChatGPT assisted me in verifying the technical accuracy of my explanations and improving grammar.

Overall, ChatGPT provided coding guidance and review assistance, while I independently conducted the experiments, evaluated the results, and documented my findings.
